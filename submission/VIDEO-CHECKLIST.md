# VeriStep demo video checklist

Target: 60-90 seconds. Record the public production site, not localhost.

1. Open https://veristep-genlayer.vercel.app/#job=v2-studio-no-fault-358323c.
2. Show Studio Next, the contract link, A/B `SATISFIED`, 3/3 verified artifacts, citations and four `DISPATCHED_UNVERIFIED` legs.
3. Open https://veristep-genlayer.vercel.app/#job=v2-studio-a-fault-r3-358323c.
4. Show A `VIOLATED`, B `SATISFIED` and the evidence explaining where the error entered the handoff.
5. Open one resolve transaction in Studio Next Explorer.
6. State that GenLayer validators judge semantic obligations while money rules are frozen in the contract.
7. State that native transfers are dispatched but contract-side receipt verification is unavailable on Studio Next.

Do not show private keys, Cloudflare/Vercel dashboards, secrets, local journals or the Portal wallet address unless intentionally public.
